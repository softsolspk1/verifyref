PJPS DATA INTEGRITY SYSTEM - MODULE 4 PAPER MILL DETECTION
Factors checked, mapped to papermill.txt
Date: 4 November 2026
=====================================================================================

HOW THE RISK LEVEL IS SET
Every factor that fires adds its weight. LOW below 4; MEDIUM at 4 or more, or any one
strong (high) signal; HIGH at 8 or more, or two strong signals. Each factor shows its
evidence (quoted text, matched record, link). Screening aid only: the editor decides.
Every check also gets a traffic light (red / amber / green / not checked).

Benchmark (150 papers retracted for paper-mill reasons vs 126 ordinary papers of the same
journals and years; text-based signals): MEDIUM or HIGH for 38/150 mill papers (25%) and
3/126 ordinary papers (2.4%). Before today: 24/150 and 2/126. Reference, author and image
signals come on top in real use.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. TEXT AND LINGUISTIC ANALYSIS                                    (papermill.txt 1)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Tortured phrases (Problematic Paper Screener + PJPS list): 1-2 / 3+    medium (2) / strong (5)
  Computer-generated text (SCIgen / Mathgen), 2+ / 4+ fingerprints       medium (3) / strong (5)
  Suspect expressions (typos, template residue)                           low (max 2)
  AI-tool leftovers ("As an AI language model", "Regenerate response")    strong (up to 6)
  AI-written text indicator (vocabulary, sentence rhythm, stock phrases)  weak (max 1)
  Template wording ("miR-X sponges Y", network-pharmacology boilerplate)  low (1) / medium (2)
  Molecular-oncology mill template (6+ of 8 features)                     medium (4)
  Technology buzzwords on a non-biomedical subject (off-scope profile)    medium (4)
  NEW: title + abstract resemble papers retracted for paper-mill
       reasons (13,447 Retraction Watch mill retractions, OpenAlex
       abstracts) - closest >= 20%: 4; >= 17%: 3; >= 14%: 2               up to medium (4)
       (title alone when no abstract index: 1-2)
  Vague methods (no supplier, catalogue no., dose, ethics approval no.)   0, or 1 if no ethics approval
  Hidden text (invisible characters, look-alike letters inside words)    medium (2) / strong (4)
  NEW: outside the PJPS scope (no pharmaceutical / biomedical subject)    shown, weight 0
  Not built: writing-style shifts inside one manuscript, comparison with an author's
  earlier papers (needs their full texts; candidates tested did not separate).

3. AUTHORSHIP, METADATA AND NETWORK                                (papermill.txt 3)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Authors with earlier retractions for paper-mill / fake-peer-review reasons
    (Retraction Watch by name)                                            strong (5)
  ORCID record lists a paper retracted for paper-mill reasons (exact DOI) strong (5)
  Other earlier retractions (name / ORCID)                                medium (2)
  ORCID iD invalid or not registered                                      medium (2)
  ORCID record with no publications; no ORCID at all                      low (1)
  Disposable (throw-away) e-mail addresses                                medium (2)

  Editorial watchlist hit (e-mail, domain, name, ORCID, institution,
    phone, phrase)                                                        weak - strong
  Affiliation unrelated to the topic                                      low (1)
  Institutions in many countries on a short author list                   low (1-2)
  Authors added / removed / reordered since the earlier version           low-medium (1-2)
  Not possible here: IP addresses and submission-system metadata (PJPS's submission
  system does not pass them to this software).

4. CITATIONS AND REFERENCES (Module 1)                             (papermill.txt 4)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  References not found in any database (possibly fabricated): 3+          strong (4)
  Cited papers retracted for paper-mill reasons                           strong (4 each, max 8)
  Cited papers flagged in published integrity audits                      low-medium
  Other retracted papers cited                                            low (1)
  Many references to journals with many paper-mill retractions            low-medium (1-2)
  Predatory / hijacked journals; citejacked list; Scopus-discontinued     low-medium
  Citation stacking: one author group / journal, citation blocks          low-medium (1-2)
  Tested, not used (no stable separation): "irrelevant" references, off-domain
  references, very recent reference lists, narrowly alike reference titles.
