2. WHAT CHANGED
---------------

Module 1 - Reference check (src/checks/reference.js)
  Previously, an invented reference with real author names was marked "Unverified -
  databases did not respond" whenever a few small databases timed out. Now, if the main
  indexes (Crossref, OpenAlex, PubMed / Europe PMC) all answered and found nothing in a
  well-indexed journal, it is marked Suspicious. This also applies when the closest record
  found is plainly a different publication.

Module 2 - PJPS style (src/checks/compliance.js)
  Now also reported: full first names ("Kenneth J. Livak" instead of "Livak KJ"), a
  missing "and" before the last author, and a missing comma after the journal name.
  Still not caught: a missing full stop after the title (it cannot be told apart from the
  journal name).

Module 3 - Claim support (src/tools/match.js)
  Spots opposite wording about the same subject ("UCP1-dependent" vs "UCP1-independent",
  "scarce" vs "expanded", "immune to" vs "hypersensitive", "ineffective" vs "improved
  outcomes"), and compares figures written in words ("one in two" vs "one in five",
  "$5 billion" vs "$750 million").
  Limit: this barely helped on the untuned half. The remaining misses need software that
  understands meaning, i.e. a language model. That would send manuscript text to an
  outside service unless the model runs locally - a decision for PJPS.

Module 4 - Paper mill (src/papermill/index.js)
  Two new signals, each tuned on half the papers and confirmed on the other half:
  - Off-topic paper: technology buzzwords applied to a non-medical subject ("big data"
    with ideological education, music, forestry economics ...). MEDIUM on its own.
  - The cancer "non-coding RNA" paper template: ncRNA "axis" title, "X via regulating Z"
    title, a stack of standard assays, standard cancer cell lines, xenograft in nude mice.
    MEDIUM at 6 or more of 8 points (13 of 150 mill papers, 0 of 126 ordinary papers).
  Both false alarms are off-topic papers in medical journals (furniture design with
  wireless sensors, building construction), which may themselves be undetected mill
  papers. Most mill papers are still only detectable through their references, authors or
  images, not their text.

Module 5 - Images (rule from version 3.2.9)
  The lower detection rate is the cost of the 80% similarity rule: about 10 marked
  duplicates that are less than 80% similar are no longer reported. Strong findings
  outside the experts' list stayed at about 1 per paper; most of those inspected look like
  real duplicates the experts did not list.
