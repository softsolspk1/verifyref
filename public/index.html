Manuscript screening for PJPS

Five modules check every submission before peer review: fabricated, wrong or retracted references, the PJPS reference style, citations that do not support the text, paper-mill signs and duplicated or edited figures.

  • 1 · Check Bibliography12 scholarly databases, DOIs, Retraction Watch, links, reference age, self-citation, predatory journals and duplicates; monitoring of published PJPS articles for retractions, corrections and concerns.
  • 2 · Reference Style & PJPSRule-by-rule PJPS style check and conversion of citations and references, in Word as Track Changes.
  • 3 · CitationsIn-text citations against the reference list, claim support from the cited sources, citation patterns and author letters in English and Urdu.
  • 4 · Paper Mill DetectionPaper-mill signals, plagiarism and text similarity, AI-written text, research data, pharmaceutical validation, ethics, authors, peer review and coordinated submissions — one explained risk score, cases and reports.
  • 5 · Image IntegrityDuplicated, rotated, mirrored, cropped, cloned, manipulated or AI-generated figures, chart reuse and re-use of earlier PJPS figures, with a forensic viewer.
© Softsols Pakistan · Manuscripts are processed for checking only and never sent to plagiarism databases or external AI services; only titles, names and identifiers are looked up in public registries.
