PJPS INTEGRITY PORTAL - FULL SCREENING WITH ALL 6 MODULES
Date: 15 November 2026 (demo with the PJPS Editor in Chief: Monday 16 November, morning)
Portal: https://verifyref.vercel.app/app
Release: commit f58427a (pushed and live on Vercel)


1. MODULE 6 (PJPS SCRIBE) IS NOW PART OF THE FULL SCREENING
-----------------------------------------------------------

- The home page card now reads "Screen a new submission - all 6 modules at once".
- PJPS Scribe appears in the Full Screening Report:
    * its own tab ("PJPS Scribe") in the report
    * row 6 on the front-page summary
    * section 6 in the Word / PDF report (Similarity, Novelty and Author
      identity are now sections 7, 8 and 9)
- The PJPS Scribe section shows:
    * the language score (0-100)
    * the suggestions per area (correctness, clarity, engagement, delivery,
      academic style)
    * each misspelt word in its sentence, with the suggested spelling
    * examples to correct
- Each screening saves a copy of the manuscript that the editor can open in
  PJPS Scribe with one click, to correct it there.
- A language score under 70 adds "return to the authors for language editing"
  to the desk-review recommendation. Language alone never triggers
  "investigate".


2. SPEED
--------

- PJPS Scribe runs at the same time as the similarity search and takes about
  10 seconds, so it does not make the report later.
- The grammar server now answers three requests at once: a 9,000-word
  manuscript takes 10 seconds instead of 20.
- Live Full Screenings take 5-12 minutes. Almost all of that time is the
  similarity search, which already runs alongside the other modules. It was
  not shortened, because searching less would lower accuracy.


3. ACCURACY FIXES (PJPS SCRIBE)
-------------------------------

Tested on four real PJPS manuscripts (scores 94-97, real typos still caught:
"fourty", "apotosis", "byseparation").

- American or British spelling is detected from the manuscript itself.
- Line breaks and line numbers from PDF files no longer produce false errors.
- Table cells, headings, spacing, quotes and dashes from the file are no
  longer reported as mistakes.
- No longer flagged:
    * author names in citations ("Kamel et al.", "Azim, 2019")
    * units and laboratory names (MPa, mmol, KBr, TRIzol)
    * cell lines and gene codes (A549, PD-1)
    * hyphenated given names (Yu-Jie, Dong-Mei)
    * "cool" used as a temperature ("store in a cool, dry place")
- American documents are no longer offered the British "favourable".


4. DATASETS ADDED (NO PAID SERVICES)
------------------------------------

PJPS Scribe
- Added the official NLM medical vocabulary (MeSH 2026: medical headings,
  drug and chemical names), free from the National Library of Medicine.
- The vocabulary now holds 368,744 terms.
- 1,666 typos left over from old PDFs were removed (for example
  "hypertention", "absense", "consistant", "softwere"), so they are caught
  again. Common medical misspellings are always reported.

Image Integrity (Module 5)
- The figure-indexing job is running overnight in its own minimised window.
- It adds about 250 papers an hour (roughly 5,000 more by Monday morning).
- New figures go live automatically.
- Please leave the laptop on and that window open.

Similarity (in-house)
- The in-house corpus already holds all 2.22 million open-access PubMed
  Central papers in the PJPS subject areas, plus all PJPS articles.
- Going further means crawling journals outside PubMed Central, which is too
  risky to start before the demo.

Paper Mill
- Nothing added; the data is already broad (Retraction Watch, Problematic
  Paper Screener, Beall's list, hijacked-journal lists, 13,417 known
  paper-mill abstracts).
- The only gap (a small share of paper-mill abstracts not yet on file) can
  only be filled with OpenAlex, which is paid and is not used from the laptop.
