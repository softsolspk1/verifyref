Reference integrity tools for editors

Fabricated references, irrelevant self-citations and finding a suitable reviewer take up much of an editor's time — at the Pakistan Journal of Pharmaceutical Sciences as at most journals. These tools are open to editor accounts only.

  • Citation patternIrrelevant self-citation and references piled on one author or one journal, before publication.
  • Reviewer FinderCandidates who recently published on the manuscript's topic, with ORCID and conflict-of-interest flags.
  • Reviewer report checkFlags reviewers' requests to cite their own work or a single journal.
  • Editor reportOne-page decision summary for desk review, a correction letter for the author and a revision comparison.
  • Issue reportSummary table of every manuscript checked in an issue: references, suspicious items and problems.
  • Reference, Citation & Journal Check12 academic databases, in-text citation matching, Scopus/WoS/HJRS indexing and predatory-journal checks.
