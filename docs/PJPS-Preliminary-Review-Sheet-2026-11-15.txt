2. HOW IT WORKS
---------------

1. Open "Preliminary Review Sheet" and upload the manuscript (Word or PDF).
   Optionally attach the authors' figure files, so their format and
   resolution can be checked.
2. The sheet is filled in automatically. Every answer shows the text it was
   read from in the manuscript.
3. Change any answer with one click. It is saved at once, and the outcome
   updates.
4. Click "Download Word form" to get the journal's own PJPS form, filled in:
   MS No, title, ticks in the boxes, the outcome, and comments listing what
   to correct.
5. All saved sheets are listed on the page, with a Word download for each.


3. WHAT IS FILLED AUTOMATICALLY
-------------------------------

MS No and title
- MS No is read from the file name (e.g. "G.P-16692" gives 16692), or can be
  typed in before uploading.
- The title is read from the manuscript.

Within the scope / Possesses Novelty / Suitable
- Scope: matched against the 19 PJPS subject areas.
- Novelty: checked against the titles of all published PJPS articles (an
  article with the same title already published is marked "No"). The deeper
  novelty check against the wider literature is still done by Full Screening.
- Suitable: follows from scope and novelty.

Article type and reporting guideline
- Type detected from the title, abstract and methods:
    * Randomized Trial (RCT) or Interventional study - CONSORT
    * Observational Study (Cohort / Case-control / Cross-sectional) - STROBE
      or RECORD
    * Case Report - CARE
    * Retrospective Study - STROBE
    * Systematic Review - PRISMA
    * Scoping Review - PRISMA-ScR
    * Animal study - ARRIVE
    * Nonrandomized / quasi-experimental intervention studies - TREND
    * Others, with a description (e.g. "In vitro / laboratory study",
      "Synthesis / medicinal chemistry", "Formulation development")
- For that type's guideline it checks whether the manuscript:
    * names the guideline (followed)
    * includes its checklist
    * has a flow diagram
- If the editor changes the type, the guideline answers switch to that
  type's guideline.

Author's Instructions for PJPS
- Abstract: structured (Background, Objectives, Methods, Results,
  Conclusion) and at most 300 words; shows the headings found and the word
  count.
- Introduction heading.
- Materials and Methods heading.
- Trial registration number and date: clinical trials only (otherwise N/A).
- Ethical approval: quotes the approval statement; human or animal studies
  only (otherwise N/A).
- Results and Discussion as separate headings ("Results and Discussion"
  combined counts as No).
- References in PJPS format: checked with the Module 2 style rules and the
  Module 3 citation check (citations missing from the list, references never
  cited).
- Figures as separate 600 dpi TIFF files: when the authors' figure files are
  attached, their format and dpi are read from the files. Pictures pasted
  inside the manuscript count as No.

Outcome
- "The article can be processed for Peer Review" when every check is met.
- "Lies in the Scope but requires resubmission after correction", listing
  what to fix.
- "Not suitable" when the article is outside the scope or the same title is
  already published in PJPS.
- The editor always decides; any answer can be changed.

5. ACCESS
---------

- Editors and Super Admins get the page automatically, including editors
  whose permissions were set before the page existed.
- Reviewers do not get it by default (a Super Admin can grant it).
- It also works in the desktop app.
- In-house checks only: no paid service is used.
