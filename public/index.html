What This Portal Audits

Every check is a live query against an authoritative public registry, eliminating human oversight and AI hallucinated references.

✕

DOIs That Resolve to Nothing

Every DOI is validated against CrossRef and the global DOI Handle System. A DOI registered with no agency does not exist — the definitive hallmark of an AI-invented citation.

⚠️

Metadata Discrepancies

Where a DOI resolves, the registered title, authors, and year are compared against what was cited. Catches swapped DOIs, author spelling errors, and year misattributions.

🚨

Retractions & Expressions of Concern

Flags whether a cited paper has been retracted or placed under an expression of concern using CrossRef and Retraction Watch updates, protecting your manuscript's credibility.

🔍

Automatic DOI Resolution

For references missing a DOI, the engine searches the CrossRef catalog to find and match the published paper, allowing one-click bibliography completion.
