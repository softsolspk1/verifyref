PJPS DATA INTEGRITY SYSTEM
Modules 1-3 (Check Bibliography, Reference Style & PJPS Converter, Citations) - Audit Report
Date: 30 October 2026
=====================================================================================

SUMMARY
-------
All three modules are fully built against the specification
(docs/PJPS-Final-5-Modules.txt): every listed feature exists in the code and works
end to end.

Accuracy was measured with the four ground-truth audits in the repository. They run
the live engine (real databases, internet) on test cases whose correct answer is
known in advance:

  Module 1  25 of 27 references judged correctly
  Module 2  14 of 14 compliance cases, 6 of 6 in-text rules, conversion from
            7 styles, Word Track Changes - all correct
  Module 3  every planted citation/list problem found; pattern flags all fired

Three defects were found (section 5): two Module 1 accuracy problems and one gap in
the audit itself. Nothing was changed in the code during this audit.

5. DEFECTS FOUND
----------------
D1  Module 1 - two miscited references passed as Verified        (accuracy)
    - Hanahan & Weinberg cited as 2012, pages 646-700 (correct: 2011, 646-674).
      The tool accepts a one-year difference as "online-first vs print year" and
      treats a wrong last page as a note, so the combination passes as Verified.
    - PRISMA 2020 statement cited as 2020, BMJ volume 370 (correct: 2021, 372).
      Verified with "year matches" - most likely matched against an earlier
      2020 version of the same paper (preprint) instead of the BMJ article.
    Effect: an author's wrong year/volume/pages is not reported to them.
    Suggested fix: when the year differs AND another field (volume or pages)
    also differs, report Miscited; prefer the journal version over a preprint
    when the cited journal is named.

D2  Module 1 - DOI with junk appended is not repaired                  (minor)
    "10.1038/nature14539.pp436-444" is kept as-is (cleanDoi in src/text.js does
    not strip the ".pp436-444" tail). The paper is still found by its title, but
    the verdict becomes Miscited ("DOI does not exist") instead of Verified with
    a repaired DOI, as the specification (1.4) requires.
    Suggested fix: strip trailing page/junk segments from a DOI when the cleaned
    DOI resolves.

D3  PDF reading - valid PDFs rejected as "damaged"                    (app defect)
    First reported as a fault in the audit's PDF generator. That was WRONG: on
    closer testing the generated PDFs are valid (every xref offset correct).
    The fault is in the app's PDF reader (pdf-parse 1.1.1 with its old pdf.js).
    Given a Node Buffer, it keeps state from the previous document, and then
    rejects about one valid PDF in three with "bad XRef entry". The same file
    reads fine in a fresh process.
    Effect: once the server has read one PDF, a later valid PDF (a manuscript in
    any module, or a source PDF in Module 3.B) could be refused as "damaged,
    password-protected or a scan".


D4  Module 3.B - a source that negates the claim counted as support  (accuracy)
    Found once D3 was fixed and uploaded PDFs could be read. Claim: the extract
    "was also active against E. coli and P. aeruginosa"; source: "No activity was
    observed against Escherichia coli or Pseudomonas aeruginosa". Verdict: Full
    support. Negation was only noticed for claims using words such as
    "significant" or "effect".


5a. FIXES APPLIED (30 October 2026)
-----------------------------------
D1  src/checks/reference.js
    - A one-year difference stays a note only when volume and pages agree; with
      a wrong volume or pages it is reported as a wrong year (Miscited).
    - A reference that names a journal is matched to the published article, not
      to a preprint of the same paper (OSF, bioRxiv, medRxiv, Research Square,
      SSRN, arXiv ...). When only a preprint exists, it is used with a note.
    - Volume, issue and pages missing from a Crossref record (BMJ deposits no
      volume) are taken from another database's record of the same DOI.
D2  src/checks/reference.js - when a DOI is not registered, text stuck on the
    end (".pp436-444", "/full", "/pdf", "/abstract", ".pdf") is removed and the
    shortened DOI is tried; the repair is reported as a note.
D3  src/parse.js, src/tools/fulltext.js - the PDF reader is given a plain
    Uint8Array copy instead of a Node Buffer: 30 of 30 valid PDFs in a row are
    now read (before: about 1 in 3 refused).
D4  src/tools/match.js - a source sentence that negates a word of the claim
    ("no activity", "did not inhibit", "failed to reduce") is a contradiction;
    when it covers the claim at least as well as the best supporting sentence,
    it decides the verdict (Unsupported). A correctly reported negative finding
    ("showed no activity against E. coli") is still Full support.

Results after the fixes
  - Module 1 audit: 27 of 27 correct (was 25 of 27).
  - Module 3.B audit: uploaded source PDF now read and linked to its reference;
    supported claim = Full, contradicted claim = Unsupported, claim with a
    figure not in the source = Unsupported, claims whose source was not
    uploaded = Could not verify (correct), retracted evidence flagged.
  - Regression tests added to test/remediation.js (npm test): DOI repair (4),
    30 PDFs in a row, 3 claim verdicts.


6. CONCLUSION
-------------
  - Modules 1, 2 and 3 are complete against the specification.
  - Module 2 and Module 3.A/3.C were correct on every test case.
  - Module 1 never called a genuine reference fake and never passed a fake one,
    but missed 2 of 4 miscited references (D1) and does not repair junk DOIs (D2).
  - Claim support from uploaded PDFs (3.B) needs a working audit before an
    accuracy figure can be given (D3).
  - Recommended next step: fix D1 and D2 (small, contained changes in the
    Module 1 matching), then repair the 3.B audit and run it again.
