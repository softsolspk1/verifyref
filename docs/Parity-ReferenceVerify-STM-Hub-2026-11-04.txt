1. SUPER ADMIN DASHBOARD - NOVELTY STATISTICS (NEW)
---------------------------------------------------
New "Novelty assessment" section on the Dashboard (Super Admin only; same period and user
filters as the rest of the page), from the novelty part (module 7) of every Full
Screening Report:
  - KPIs: manuscripts assessed, average and median score, high / moderate / low novelty
    (count and %), average similarity to the closest published work, reports that could
    not be assessed
  - Chart: score distribution (ten 10-point bars, coloured by novelty band)
  - Stacked bars: novelty band; 5-year citation outlook; confidence of the assessment
  - Table: the 100 most recent assessments - date, user, manuscript, score, band,
    similarity to the closest work (with its title), close works in the last 5 years,
    citation outlook, Open link
Storage: the report stub kept in the database (reports moved to R2) now carries the
novelty summary and the closest work. Reports moved to R2 before today are read from R2
once (40 per page load) and their stub is filled in, so old reports appear without a
migration.
Files: src/routes/activity.js, src/reportStore.js, public/js/views/dashboard.js,
public/css/app.css. Test: test/reports-dashboard.js (full result, R2 stub, not assessed,
not yet back-filled, user filter).

Benchmark of the new signal (150 papers retracted for paper-mill reasons vs 126 ordinary
papers of the same journals and years; each paper's own record left out of the index):
  title similarity >= 0.5 to a mill retraction:   15/150 mill vs 4/126 ordinary
  title similarity >= 0.6:                         2/150 vs 0/126
  3 or more look-alikes >= 0.4:                   12/150 vs 3/126
Weights set from these rates: LOW (1) at >= 0.5; MEDIUM (2) at >= 0.6 or 3+ look-alikes.
On the text-only benchmark it changes no paper's level (MEDIUM+ stays 24/150 mill,
2/126 ordinary); in real use it adds to the reference, author and image signals. The
thresholds were read on the full benchmark (no held-out half), so treat them as a first
setting.
Files: src/papermill/index.js (millLookalikes), src/papermill/overview.js,
src/datasets.js. Test: test/papermill-signals.js (34 checks).


4. WHAT IS STILL NOT THE SAME
-----------------------------
a) PubPeer: needs PJPS's API key.
b) Cross-publisher duplicate submissions: needs STM Integrity Hub membership.
c) Clear Skies compares against its own private collection of mill papers (including
   unretracted ones); PJPS uses the public Retraction Watch list, so it sees only mill
   papers that have been retracted.
d) ReferenceVerify's Word add-in: not built.
