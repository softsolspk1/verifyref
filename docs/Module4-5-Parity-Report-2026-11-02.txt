PJPS DATA INTEGRITY SYSTEM
Module 4 (Paper Mill) and Module 5 (Image Integrity) compared with
STM Integrity Hub, Proofig AI and ImageTwin
Date: 2 November 2026
=====================================================================================

SUMMARY
-------
Every published feature of the three products was listed and compared with Modules 4
and 5. All gaps that can be built inside PJPS have now been built and tested on the web
portal (the desktop app gets them with its next build, because it uses the same code).

Three things cannot be copied by software alone, because they are data or shared
membership, not features. They are listed at the end.

Sources: the ImageTwin and Proofig user manuals and product pages (saved in data/new),
and public STM Integrity Hub descriptions (STM, Scholarly Kitchen, Editage interview).

3. WHAT CANNOT BE MADE "100% THE SAME"
--------------------------------------
a) The 155-160 million figure databases of Proofig and ImageTwin. They are licensed
   from publishers. PJPS compares with its own databases (PJPS archive, PubMed Central
   open access, retracted papers, RSIID, BioFors, BINDER). A figure copied from a
   paywalled paper that is in none of these is not found. Only a vendor contract closes
   this, and PJPS policy currently keeps figures in-house.
b) Cross-publisher duplicate submissions (STM Integrity Hub). This needs Hub
   membership: the other publishers' submissions are only visible to members.
   PJPS detects duplicates among its own submissions.
c) Trained AI-image classifiers. Proofig and ImageTwin use models trained on hundreds
   of thousands of images. PJPS uses file markers (strong evidence) and statistics
   (indicators only). The generator is named only when the file itself names it;
   PJPS does not guess one.

No tool in this group finds every problem. ImageTwin states 80-90 % detection. PJPS
benchmark figures are in docs/benchmark-images.md and docs/benchmark-papermill.md.
